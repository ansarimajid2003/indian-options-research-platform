--- STRATEGY A: NIFTY SHORT STRANGLE (INTRADAY) ---

MARKET VIEW: Range-bound / Neutral
CAPITAL ALLOCATION: 40% of portfolio
OPTIMAL VIX RANGE: 13-22

SETUP RULES:
1. Entry Time: 9:40 AM (after opening volatility settles)
2. Exit Time: 3:15 PM (fixed time exit)
3. Expiry: Trade only on DTE-3 to DTE-0 (Tue/Wed expiry week)
4. Strike Selection: Sell OTM options 2 strikes away from ATM
   - Example: Nifty at 24,000 → Sell 24,200 CE & 23,800 PE
5. Stop Loss: 1.2% of capital per leg OR 40% of premium per leg
6. Do NOT hold overnight

BACKTESTED DATA (Source: finance.anuraggoel.in, 6-month Nifty study):
- Capital per lot: Rs.70,000
- Total ROI over 124 trading days: 17.03%
- Monthly Average ROI: 2.88%
- Win Rate: 64.52% (80 wins out of 124 days)
- Average Profit per winning day: Rs.597
- Average Loss per losing day: Rs.815
- Maximum Profit in a day: Rs.2,444
- Maximum Loss in a day: Rs.1,540
- Max Drawdown: Rs.4,153 (5.09%)
- Sharpe Ratio: 1.96
- Sortino Ratio: 7.22
- Friday showed negative returns (avoid or reduce size)

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Risk per trade: 1.5% of allocated capital
- Max 2 lots per Rs.1.5L allocated capital
- If VIX > 25, skip trading that day
- Avoid Budget day, RBI policy day, US Fed days

EXPECTED RETURNS:
- Conservative: 2.0% per month (24% annualized)
- Base case: 2.88% per month (35% annualized)
- Optimistic: 3.5% per month (42% annualized)

--- STRATEGY B: NIFTY IRON CONDOR (POSITIONAL) ---

MARKET VIEW: Range-bound / Neutral with defined risk
CAPITAL ALLOCATION: 40% of portfolio
OPTIMAL VIX RANGE: 15-25

SETUP RULES:
1. Entry: 18-24 days before monthly expiry (Tuesday)
2. Exit: At 50% profit OR 3 days before expiry (whichever first)
3. Strike Selection:
   - Sell CE: ATM + 200-300 points (delta ~0.15-0.20)
   - Sell PE: ATM - 200-300 points (delta ~0.15-0.20)
   - Buy CE: Sell strike + 200 points (protection)
   - Buy PE: Sell strike - 200 points (protection)
4. Example at Nifty 24,000:
   - Sell 24,300 CE @ Rs.80
   - Buy 24,500 CE @ Rs.35
   - Sell 23,700 PE @ Rs.75
   - Buy 23,500 PE @ Rs.30
   - Net Credit: Rs.90 per unit = Rs.5,850 per lot (90 x 65)

BACKTESTED DATA:
- Win Rate: 65-75% (true range-bound markets)
- Capital required: ~Rs.80,000-1,20,000 per lot (defined risk = lower margin)
- Monthly Returns: 3-8% on deployed capital
- Max Loss per trade: Limited to spread width minus credit
- Max Drawdown: 8-12% historically
- Research study (Apr 2020 - Mar 2021, Quantman backtest):
  * Short Iron Condor on Nifty monthly: 3 out of 4 variations profitable
  * Long Iron Condor on Nifty weekly: All 4 variations profitable

ADJUSTMENT RULES:
- If Nifty moves within 100 points of sold strike, roll the threatened side
- If 50% profit reached in first week, close and redeploy
- If loss reaches 2x credit received, exit completely

EXPECTED RETURNS:
- Conservative: 3.0% per month (36% annualized)
- Base case: 5.0% per month (60% annualized)
- Optimistic: 8.0% per month (96% annualized - rare, requires wide wings)

--- STRATEGY C: BANK NIFTY MODIFIED SHORT STRADDLE ---

MARKET VIEW: Captures theta decay with trend-day adaptation
CAPITAL ALLOCATION: 20% of portfolio
NOTE: Bank Nifty weekly options discontinued; this is adapted for monthly

SETUP RULES (The "Optimized 9:20" Adaptation):
1. For Monday (trending day tendency):
   - Entry: 9:30 AM
   - Sell ATM CE + PE
   - Stop Loss: 15% per leg (tight, lets winners run)
2. For Tue-Fri (range-bound tendency):
   - Entry: 9:18 AM (capture higher opening premium)
   - Sell ATM CE + PE
   - Stop Loss: 70% per leg (wide, avoid whipsaws)
3. Exit: 3:15 PM fixed
4. Only trade DTE-7 to DTE-0 of monthly expiry

BACKTESTED DATA (Source: Medium/Algotest, 5-year Bank Nifty study):
- Average Capital: Rs.90,000
- Total Profit: Rs.5,34,000 (593% absolute return)
- Win Rate: 69%
- Max Drawdown: ~Rs.17,000 (~19%)
- Only 9 losing months out of 60
- Profit not reinvested in backtest; compound returns would be higher
- CAGR equivalent: ~47%

CRITICAL MODIFICATIONS FROM ORIGINAL 9:20:
- Original 9:20 with 50% SL FAILED in 2021-2022 (105-day drawdown)
- Moving entry to 9:16 AM with 25% SL showed drastic improvement
- Monday = trending (tight SL), Other days = range (wide SL)

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Max 2 lots on Rs.2L allocated capital
- If both legs hit SL, skip next trading day (cooling off)
- Reduce size by 50% if VIX > 22

EXPECTED RETURNS:

SECTION 4: INDIA VIX - THE VOLATILITY FILTER
================================================================================

India VIX is the most critical filter for all three strategies.

VIX RANGE     | MARKET CONDITION        | ACTION
--------------|-------------------------|----------------------------------
< 10          | Extremely calm          | Premiums too low; avoid selling
10 - 13       | Low volatility          | Reduce size by 50%; tight stops
13 - 17       | Normal (GOLDILOCKS)     | FULL SIZE - optimal for selling
17 - 22       | Elevated                | Good for selling; use wider wings
22 - 30       | High/Stress             | Reduce size 50%; or pause
> 30          | Extreme/Crisis          | STOP TRADING completely

HISTORICAL CONTEXT:
- Lowest VIX: ~10.14 (July 2023)
- Highest VIX: >85 (Nov 2008 GFC, Mar 2020 COVID)
- Average VIX (2022-2024): ~16-20
- VIX tends to mean-revert; spikes >30 usually short-lived

EVENT CALENDAR - NO TRADE DAYS:
- Union Budget day (Feb 1)
- RBI Monetary Policy days (6-8 times/year)
- US Fed meeting days (8 times/year)
- Election result days
- Major war/geopolitical escalation days
- Monthly expiry day (Tuesday) after 2 PM for new positions

SECTION 7: CRITICAL RISK MANAGEMENT RULES
================================================================================

THESE ARE NON-NEGOTIABLE:

1. STOP LOSSES ARE MANDATORY
   - Every trade MUST have a predefined stop loss
   - Honor stops without exception - no "waiting for recovery"

2. MAXIMUM DRAWDOWN LIMITS
   - Strategy-level: 8% max drawdown = halt and review
   - Portfolio-level: 15% max drawdown = half positions
   - Portfolio-level: 25% max drawdown = stop completely

3. NO OVERNIGHT EXCEPT IRON CONDORS
   - Short straddles/strangles: NEVER hold overnight
   - Iron condors: Only defined-risk positions held overnight

4. EVENT RISK PROTOCOL
   - Check economic calendar every morning
   - Reduce size by 50% on event-heavy weeks
   - Full cash on Budget/Fed/RBI days

5. VOLATILITY PROTOCOL
   - VIX > 25: Reduce size by 50%
   - VIX > 30: No new positions
   - VIX > 40: Close all positions, go to cash

6. CONSECUTIVE LOSS RULE
   - 3 consecutive losing days: Reduce size by 50%
   - 5 consecutive losing days: Stop for the week

7. MONTHLY REVIEW
   - Calculate win rate, average profit/loss, max drawdown
   - If win rate drops below 55% for 2 months, re-evaluate strategy
   - Adjust strike selection or stop losses based on recent data

FINAL WORDS
================================================================================

This strategy is NOT a get-rich-quick scheme. It is a systematic, 
probabilistic approach to capturing option time decay in India's 
highly liquid index options market.

The math works in your favor:
- Time decay is guaranteed (theta > 0 for sellers)
- High win rates (65-70%) compound over time
- Defined risk structures (iron condors) prevent catastrophe
- Volatility filtering (VIX) keeps you out of danger zones

However, discipline is everything. The strategy that returns 40% annually 
in backtests often returns -20% for undisciplined traders who:
- Skip stop losses
- Hold losing positions overnight
- Trade on event days
- Size too large for their capital
- Chase losses with revenge trading

Your edge is not the strategy itself. Your edge is your ability to 
execute the strategy with mechanical discipline, day after day, 
month after month.
